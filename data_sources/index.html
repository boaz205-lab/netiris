    1. Communications Authority of Kenya (National KE-CIRT/CC)

    • Source Name: Communications Authority of Kenya (CA) / National KE-CIRT/CC
    • Report Title: Cyber Security Report Q1 2026-2027
    • Link: https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-10Cyber%20Security%20Report%20Q1%202026-2027.pdf
    • Date Checked: November 2, 2026
    • License/Attribution: Public government report; requires attribution to the Communications Authority of Kenya.
    • Key Findings: Detected national cyber threat events rose by 14.45% in a single quarter (July–September 2026) to reach 2.696 billion. Social engineering, stolen credentials, and malicious links targeting mobile ecosystems drove a 34.43% surge in issued mobile threat advisories.

    2. INTERPOL African Cyberthreat Assessment Report

    • Source Name: INTERPOL Africa Cybercrime Operations Desk
    • Report Title: African Cyberthreat Assessment Report 2026
    • Link: https://www.interpol.int/Media/Documents/Publications/Cybercrime/African-Cyberthreat-Assessment-Report-2026
    • Date Checked: November 2, 2026
    • License/Attribution: Publicly available report; requires clear attribution to INTERPOL.
    • Key Findings: Generative AI tools are actively enabling up to 55% of all cybercrime across Africa. Social engineering, smishing (SMS phishing), and synthetic identity fraud are specifically targeting mobile financial services and public sector institutions.
