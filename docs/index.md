## Providing intel on cybersecurity risks across African continent and third world countries

Welcome to **NetIris**, your overview of everything cybersecurity.

As digital adoption grows, especially in mobile money and social media, cyber risks aren't left behind. NetIris provides plain-language guides and aggregated threat data to help you understand these threats and protect yourself.

## Why NetIris?

Cybersecurity information is often for large corporations or advanced professionals or advanced IT professionals. **NetIris** bridges that gap.

- **For Students and Teachers**: Understand the digital risks you face daily on campus and online.

- **For Small Businesses**: Protect your mobile money, customer data, and digital presence from fraud.

- **For Journalists And Researchers**: Access verified, cited data on the cyber threat landscape in Africa and Third World Countries.
